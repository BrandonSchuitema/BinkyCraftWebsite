Devlog Fourteen - Mobs and Enemies

Devlog Fifteen Overview

This devlog update really brings the world to life. Not only is there plant life throughtout the world, but now there is also animal life. The movement for everything is still pretty janky though. But that'll get smoothed out in time as I perfect stuff and figure out exactly how I want everything to move and operate.

Extra text for something I was going to talk about, but I forgot what it was right after.

Main Features

The first feature I'm going to talk about will of course be the natural spawning of mobs throughout the world. I'll quickly go into how that works. The player is the center of attention here. Within a set range of the player, the mob spawner will choose a random spot. Then it'll check that spot for conditions. Is there any mob that can spawn in this biome the spot is in? Yes. Ok good. Is it in the terrain? Choose again. Is it fully in air? Good. Now bring it down to a good height for that mob to spawn on the ground.

And now that the mobs can spawn naturally in the world, I finally added some very basic for all the mob models I have currently in the game. The mobs will of course just spawn and move about randomly though. The enemies though like the Zombie will try to follow the player and attack them.

Attacking brings me to my next feature. The player can now attack mobs, and mobs can now attack the player.

Blocks

The biomes will of course have a whole bunch of new blocks to them. This first segment of blocks will be everything found in the Jungle Biome. So here are the new Jungle Wood blocks, and a picture of the Jungle Biome again. You can check out the new Bamboo pretty good in that.

Recipes

Given the nature of this devlog update, I thought it time to start really working on the recipes in the game. Because I'll be honest: I haven't given many items a crafting recipe. Literally the only way to get them is in the creative menu. So I'm gonna start really working on this. So here is a bunch of recipes for items in the game. Just to give you a taste.

Oh, and I'm also slowly working on a Recipe page for the website. I've decided I don't want a recipe book in the game. But I am fine with having somewhere online for people to search up how to craft the items. Oh, and I took those pictures before I did the work on the item info pop up. Just in case you had any thoughts about that.

Conclusion

This devlog update took a little longer to release, and I'm fine with that. There's a bit more content in this update. At least it took longer to make the content. And overall, I'm happy with the update.

Now that the world is starting to look like it's actually alive, maybe it's time to start properly adding in actual life. Not just plants, but things moving around. I'm not saying that's the next update, but it's possible. At the very least, I'm going to start working away at it on the side. I do know for sure that the next update will continue making the game feel more complete.

Side topic because I want to talk about this. I was thinking what I would do if someone offered to help work on this project with me. Would I be happy for the help? Of course. But would I actually let them help? I'm not sure. There's a lot of work to be done, but this project is also a learning excersize for me, and also a sort of passion project. I honestly think I'd turn down their help. That is unless their help was better textures, UI, and/ or sounds. Those are all things that I could be much better, and I don't have the talent for.
